# Innsikt

**Outcome:** `pulse innsikt` delivers `moment: the-noticing` — one concrete observation from the user's own journal, citing real days and values, plus one gentle question. Local model only (NorMistral via ollama); the journal never leaves the machine.

**How I'll know it works:** the proving scenario from product.md — a journal with planted low Mondays must get an observation naming that pattern with real days; a patternless journal must not produce a claimed pattern; output citing days not in the journal is the worst defect and the validator must catch it; ollama missing or timing out gets a plain honest message; the wait is named up front. Judged against the good/unacceptable examples in product.md.

**Status:** building.
