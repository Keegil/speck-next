## Product synthesis

The roles agree on the product direction and disagree usefully about what could make it fail. Product resolves the design this way:

- The four roles run in distinct contexts once before Shape closes, once before Map closes, and once before code for every substantial piece. A current carrier may return; a heading or persona switch inside Product does not count.
- Each contribution records carrier, direct evidence, conclusion, assumptions, proposed change, and active decision. A role claiming material impact must name the consequence and the earliest run that could disprove it. Product cannot mark that concern inactive, but still resolves the product tradeoff.
- Product shows the owner one integrated recommendation. Dissent reaches the owner only when it changes a promise, user-facing choice, risk, or order. The work file preserves the rest as consequence, evidence, and settling run.
- Experience is the standing product-building responsibility. Lowercase experience remains the later fresh-user phase. An Experience contributor forms product hypotheses and never stands in for a user.
- Business rules `kept / broken / not judged` on changes to users, value, price or revenue, acquisition or adoption, ongoing cost, or durable value, and on every milestone. `broken` and `not judged` block the affected piece or milestone. This is conditional business evidence, not a fifth universal quality ruling.
- Active roles return at the first real run and record what the run showed, what changed or held, and the resulting product change. A consequential miss after a role marked its concern handled keeps that role continuously involved in the next piece; a repeat keeps it through the milestone until evidence supports relaxing it.
- Role contributors are ineligible as fresh testers and judges. The later review sees product promises and the running product, not the role conclusions.
- Existing marker-bearing repositories receive an honest unassessed product-team section exactly once. Historical work is untouched. Four separate contexts assess the product before the next substantial piece, reopening Shape only for a wrong promise.
- The implementation stays inside existing files and caps. It introduces no skill, installed file, state dashboard, owner sign-off beat, or new universal verdict.

### Preserved dissent

- **Field value remains unproven.** Kernel evidence can prove mechanics, portability, owner-attention cost, and whether the fixture changes a decision. It cannot prove durable value in a real product. `capabilities.md` must say so and name the next real milestone as the settling run.
- **Role records can become forms.** The consequence would be duplicate prose and slower first runs. The cross-cutting task and milestone testers settle this by checking for different actions, a builder-ready handoff, and one owner-facing recommendation.
- **Structured logs may not prove nested carrier identity equally on both hosts.** The consequence would be a green based on self-report. Engineering must normalize host-issued evidence per driver; a planted one-carrier record is the control.
