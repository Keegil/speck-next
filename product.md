# The product here is Speck Next itself

We're building the small kernel that runs repositories like this one: the method page (`AGENTS.md`), a handful of skills, the upgrader from old Speck, and the state generator. It's for one owner and his agents, across all his product repositories.

What makes it good, and the promises that define success, live in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) — eight promises, each with a check that can fail. This repository runs under its own method: kernel work follows the loop, gets proven by running, gets independently reviewed, and lands in `state.md` honestly.

## Product team

- **Product** protects the owner's product-building intent, integrates the other roles, and keeps the product, map, decisions, and work records true. Product does not implement the installed method.
- **Business** protects useful adoption, operating cost, and durable value. It judges whether the separated team earns the added context cost.
- **Experience** protects the owner's and builder's ability to understand and drive the method without carrying its internal machinery.
- **Engineering** owns the installed method, templates, installer, migration, development suite, and release integrity.

There is no standing specialist. A specialist joins only when a product-specific risk gives it one responsibility and an observable exit trigger.
