# Pulse

A one-command energy journal for one person. `pulse 4` logs today's energy (1–5); `pulse` shows the last 14 days as a tiny chart in the terminal.

**Who it's for:** someone who wants to notice their energy pattern without an app, an account, or a habit heavier than typing one word.

**What makes it good:** logging takes under a second · the chart is honest — days you skipped show as gaps, never smoothed over · zero setup, zero dependencies.

**Promises:** a logged day is never lost · the chart never invents data.
