# v3 — full sequence discipline

**Outcome:** the loop becomes four phases with hard contracts — shape → map → build → prove — where "where are we and is it done" is always checkable: each phase has an entry condition, a named owner conversation or procedure, canonical artifacts with templates, and an exit that requires the owner's ratification in his own words. Work is resolved: a piece is an instance inside build, born in the map, carrying one work file cradle-to-grave through its own micro-loop. Transitions are dispatcher rules in the method page — automatic, fluid, always named out loud, with re-entry into earlier phases as a first-class traced move. Mapping becomes a conversation (`map-build`: rounds, verbatim record, the substrate round, a mechanical completion test). The conductor protocol is law: push-by-default, where-we-are in every reply, decisions as options with costs and a recommendation, bad news first. Shaped material is first-class — any artifact a product needs (a domain model, a journey study), with full citizenship in the map's accounting and the review law. Review receipts open at dispatch or they are void.

**How I'll know it works:** the owner's own bar, stated after the mapping failure — he steers every phase the way he steered shaping: visible rounds, his words on record, no moment where he has to ask what process is running or where anything is. Measured on Odd's next sessions: the dispatcher names the phase unprompted, mapping re-runs as rounds and exits through the completion test plus his ratification, the substrate decision lands in its own round at a stated care level, and every review receipt exists from the moment of dispatch.
