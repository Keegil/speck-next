## Triaged defects

1. **Breaks a promise — the witness is told to judge.** `experience/references/walk.md` says “Judge the recovery” and requires “broken” in the record, directly contradicting the no-verdict stance; `experience/SKILL.md` also frames the worker's mechanism trace as “proving”; `map-build/references/questions.md` retains a reviewer attack role. **Route: build. Files:** `.claude/skills/experience/references/walk.md`, `.claude/skills/experience/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/map-build/references/questions.md`. Rewrite every gathering instruction as an observation to record; leave the ruling solely to `judge`. ([record 72–92](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L72-L92), [record 513–524](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L513-L524))

2. **Real defect — the judge's own boundary contains an execution-shaped ambiguity.** “Walk every promise” forced the witness to infer that the judge traces records rather than runs the product. The stronger explicit prohibitions keep the promise intact, but this sentence makes the most important boundary harder to follow. **Route: build. File:** `.claude/skills/judge/SKILL.md`. Say “trace every promise through the records and attached evidence.” ([record 526–537](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L526-L537))

3. **Breaks a promise — the owner-facing method is not understandable in one read.** Undefined or delayed terms, a 246-word build-loop sentence, unclear “re-land”/derivation actions, generic review language, owner-versus-judge ambiguity, and contradictory summaries made the cold reader stop and infer. **Route: build. Files:** `AGENTS.md` and `README.md`. Re-render the same method in plain product language without changing its decisions. ([record 26–60](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L26-L60))

4. **Breaks a promise — phase ratification has no coherent v4 meaning.** The contract and README say every phase exits with owner ratification, while Build and Experience do not, Judge names an owner taste grade rather than a ratification quote, and the piece template has no owner-ratification field. This is not safely repairable by picking one text: the split created a new owner-touchpoint question. **Route: shaping. File that must change first:** `CONTRACT.md`; after the owner rules what ratification means for Build, Experience, and Judge, reconcile `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, and `templates/piece.md` to that promise. ([record 584–594](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L584-L594))

5. **Real defect — the receipt skeletons omit mandatory fields.** The piece template omits planned commands and refers to “the four” without naming them; the rounds template used by Shape and Map has no witness or judge receipt despite both exits requiring them. **Route: build. Files:** `templates/piece.md` and `templates/rounds.md`. ([record 197–215](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L197-L215))

6. **Real defect — the README narrows the method while explaining it.** Its example says “nothing open” after naming only three of four verdicts, and its Experience summary omits the second-user persona. **Route: build. File:** `README.md`. ([record 50–53](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L50-L53), [record 547–575](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L547-L575))

7. **Polish — the installed-file count has an unstated boundary.** The installer and README say 18, while the installed repository contains 19 regular files including `.claude/speck-next.json`; either count remains within 20. **Route: build. Files:** `bin/speck-next.js` and `README.md`; name whether the marker is included, then use that boundary consistently. ([record 349–420](docs/reviews/v4-witness-record.md#L349-L420))
