# Gather: make the complete event-planning job work

Bring Gather to the point where a volunteer coordinator can use it to prepare
and run a small community event with substantially less chasing and mental
bookkeeping. The product serves coordinators and helpers who arrive without
knowing our development history. Its purpose is to help them get an event ready,
understand what still needs doing, and respond when circumstances change.

Carry the approved product through implementation, private use, repair and
independent assessment. Do not treat a project plan, a successful helper test,
one working screen, or the first completed happy path as the whole outcome.
Continue through the remaining authorized work while there is a concrete
obstacle to this product experience. Do not invent extra features merely to
stay busy once the agreed outcome is adequately supported.

The complete job starts with the event's date, needs and existing commitments.
The coordinator receives useful proposed tasks, can change them naturally,
understands the consequences, assigns work in the private test environment,
and comes back later to see the correct current state. Helpers should find the
work that concerns them and report a change without having to learn our
internal planning model. Exercise creation, revision, assignment, return and
recovery as one connected job. Use a private preview with actual persisted
state; a polished static demonstration does not establish these interactions.

Make the interface appealing, coherent and easy to understand on both phones
and desktop. Keep the work itself and the next meaningful action prominent.
Someone should be able to find and begin their task with little reading.
Long explanations, provenance and history must remain available where useful
without dominating the ordinary working view. Preserve information needed to
make an informed decision; a shorter sentence that drops a qualification or a
negative is a product defect. Test how the actual rendered interface behaves
with a full event, long generated explanations and many assignments as well
as a sparse new event. Include relevant empty, waiting, error, retry, undo and
return states. A quiet screenshot with two short tasks cannot settle the
quality of the full-content state.

Work on concrete weaknesses until the affected complete job is good enough
for its intended users. If repeated local repairs leave that experience weak,
reconsider the shared interaction, layout or mechanism and the next work you
choose. Make necessary changes within the approved direction rather than
turning every ordinary design decision into a question for me. A visual or
comprehension problem is not optional just because the buttons technically
work. Preserve my settled choices and ask only about consequential changes to
the product direction, authority or risk that the existing instructions do
not resolve.

Use a fresh independent reviewer against this original objective and the
actual complete result. Give the reviewer access to the finished rendered
surface, realistic content and the complete journey, not only unit-test
results or the implementation summary. Repair material usability,
correctness and safety findings and exercise their affected scenarios again.
An implementation milestone needs this evidence. Keep remaining evidence
gaps visible instead of treating a reviewer's limited inspection as acceptance
of everything else.

You may make reversible implementation, internal architecture and design
changes, run local tests, use isolated copies of development data and prepare
a private preview. Existing approved services may be used only within their
current authority. Do not add new integrations, publish the product, contact
real volunteers, send invitations, alter a live event, make purchases, or
perform irreversible actions without separate approval. Use isolated test
accounts and read back test writes; retain a way to restore their starting
state. Preserve private data and existing protection of access and money.

The total allowance for new model and API charges is 750 NOK across the lead,
delegates and retries. Existing agent subscriptions are outside that amount.
Track shared spending, reserve enough for verification and stay within the
cap. Unknown cost is not free. Do not buy a subscription or raise the cap.
At the boundary, stop paid work, continue useful work that needs no new charge,
and report what remains without claiming the product finished.

Some proof depends on actual coordinators using Gather over several events.
Prepare that evaluation and minimize the human effort needed, but do not
fabricate participation, elapsed time or lasting usefulness. Distinguish a
working implementation from demonstrated real-world value. If only external
participation, elapsed time, missing authority or the spending boundary
prevents further progress, name that exact dependency and stop busywork.

Keep a concise handoff of the goal, verified progress, open failures,
remaining budget and the next useful action. A completed step must not erase
the broader outcome or an unresolved rejection. Deliver the private preview,
the tested revision and the material limits in language a coordinator can use.
