# Shaping — separated product team

This append-only record reopened Shape because the method carried phases and review roles, but no separated product-building responsibilities from first promise through the running product. Concrete consumer-product evidence stays in its private repository; this public record keeps only the abstract method decision and safe owner words.

## Round 1 — the missing product team

**Owner, verbatim:**

> Bro! We need to talk about introducing the concept of product-building roles to Speck Next. I know we don't want to be very careful with introducing a lot of new shit, but I'm really feeling the lack of different lenses when the products are being taken throughout the entire process. The methdology seems to lose track of business, product, UX and technical aspects, and so on sometimes, and don't seem to have a structured way to ensure these are taken care of. Please let's think thoroughly and riff on this.

The first cut treated a role as a persistent responsibility that one carrier could hold, with separate specialists only after a miss. The owner selected the four standing responsibilities — Product, Business, Experience and Engineering — with product-specific specialists added only when the product creates the need. Product integrates reversible tradeoffs; the owner decides changed promises, priorities, business bets and deliberate trades against whole-product properties. Every substantial piece lets all four inspect its impact, then keeps the materially affected roles active. Business is judged on business-changing pieces and every milestone. Meaningful role disagreement reaches the owner as distinct views followed by Product's recommendation.

## Round 2 — the separation question

**Owner, verbatim:**

> Hmm, let's dwell a bit more on the separation. I'm wondering if our experience with both Speck and Speck Next would argue for a stronger separation as in 2. Can we think about and discuss this?

The field record changed the design. Repeated author blind spots showed that one context switching headings improves recall but does not produce a different frame. The predecessor's many document reviewers showed the opposite limit: separation without a different reference and medium multiplies the same lens. The revised design therefore separates contexts, responsibilities, primary evidence and product outputs.

The owner selected:

- **Separate at key beats.** Four distinct contexts shape, map, inspect every substantial piece before code, and return at its first real run when materially affected. Engineering carries implementation between those beats.
- **Distinct context required.** Models or vendors may differ, but the method cannot claim separated roles when contexts are shared.
- **Next substantial piece.** Existing products adopt the team before their next substantial build; they reopen Shape only if the separated assessment finds a wrong promise.
- **Measured growth allowed.** The release adds no installed file or skill. Clear role guidance may grow inside the standing limits when the evidence shows it earns its place.
- **Kernel-only release evidence.** A live consumer-product pilot is not a prerequisite for v6. The first production adoption follows release at a safe boundary.

## Round 3 — ratified direction

The owner saw the following promise in full:

> **Speck builds every substantial piece with a separated product team. Product, Business, Experience, and Engineering each work in a distinct context and read their own primary evidence. Product conducts and integrates but does not build; Engineering owns implementation. All four contribute separately during shaping and mapping, and scan every piece before code. The roles materially affected by that piece return at its first real run, while choices are still cheap. Product preserves their different conclusions and assumptions, makes reversible tradeoffs, and shows the owner meaningful divergence plus one recommendation. Business is explicitly judged on every business-changing piece and every milestone. Product-specific specialists join only with one responsibility and an exit trigger. Role contributors never count as fresh testers or judges. Small changes remain exempt. A consequential miss after a role marked its concern handled makes that role continuously involved on the next piece; a repeat keeps that separation in force until a milestone proves it can safely relax. Existing products adopt the team before their next substantial piece and reopen shaping only if the separated scan finds a wrong promise.**

The options shown were:

- **Yes, lock v6 (Recommended):** Treat this as the complete product direction and produce the implementation-ready plan for a kernel-only v6 release.
- **Adjust the promise:** Keep shaping because one of the role boundaries, beats, or escalation rules still needs changing.
- **Return to live pilot:** Restore a live consumer product as a pre-release proving subject before the method becomes permanent.

**Owner selection, verbatim:**

> Yes, lock v6 (Recommended)

This ratifies the direction, not an unreviewed wording change elsewhere. The product and contract edits still receive their own fresh test, judgment and plain-language owner rendering before Shape closes.

## Round 4 — redesign after the running cost failure

The bounded run stopped after Product and one Business contribution consumed 266,484 of the 250,000-token allowance. It had not reached two role contributions, synthesis, implementation, or the first real product run. Business ruled the execution design broken. This measured finding reopens Shape because “four separate contexts at every substantial piece” may itself be the wrong promise rather than only a bad carrier implementation.

**Owner, verbatim:**

> Alright, let's redesign a bit, but also consider if it's worth adjusting the rules a bit if we think we can make things genuinely better.

Product is asking separate Business, Experience, and Engineering contexts to challenge both levels: which lenses must never disappear, and at which decisions a separate carrier actually improves the product enough to earn its cost. No implementation changes until Product integrates those contributions and the owner sees the resulting product promise in plain language. The reopening decision is recorded in `decisions.md` on 2026-09-02.

### Four separate contributions

**Product — carrier `/root` — active.** Product read the owner's original concern, the previously ratified promise, the five governed attempts, the stopped run's raw metrics, the three new role contributions, and the eight contract promises. The roles did not fail as lenses: in the only run that reached a product decision, Business rejected an unsupported price, Experience removed completion pressure, Engineering found the shared implementation seam, and Product combined those into the right behavior before code. The universal cadence failed: it made four separate contexts a prerequisite at every phase and piece even when a role's evidence could not change the decision. Product assumes the method must protect independent judgment, not maximize role appearances. Proposed change: keep four fixed responsibilities, make Product and Engineering the separate floor for every substantial piece, and let Business and Experience own observable conditions that summon them. Keep the full four-context assessment where the product's promises and first build order are set. Product cannot waive a fired condition or a role's declaration that it is materially affected.

**Business — replacement carrier `/root/business_cost_return` — active.** Business read the current method and contract, the role broker's seven mandatory stages, run 4's distinct product actions, and run 5's host metrics. Run 5 spent 266,484 gross tokens in 110 seconds after only Product and Business; 210,432 input tokens were cache reads, while 56,052 tokens were uncached input plus output. The stop was honest, but the default could not deliver enough product to justify its operating cost. Business assumes observable call conditions can be authored when the product is shaped and applied to mapped pieces, and that a focused replacement carrier may continue the evidence without inheriting a large session. Proposed change: one full separated assessment at initial or reopened Shape and the initial Map; thereafter Product and Engineering always, with Business called when market, value, price, revenue, adoption, material ongoing cost, or durable value can change, and Experience called when a user-facing journey, surface, behavior, comprehension, accessibility, trust, or declared feel can change. Ambiguity fires the relevant role. Business remains binding on every milestone and every business-changing piece. A missed inactive-role condition broadens the condition and forces that role onto the next comparable piece. Business rules the redesign **kept — worth building, not yet releasable**; this permits method edits and component controls after ratification, not another end-to-end run yet.

**Experience — carrier `/root/redesign_experience_role` — active.** Experience read the installed role rules, the fixed four-row piece and map templates, the owner's original words, and the two governed runs that reached the real role mechanism. The fixed rows reward complete-looking departmental prose and the all-role barrier delays the first product evidence. “Return at the first real run” is also too blunt: a technical smoke run may say nothing about comprehension or adoption. Experience assumes the goal is timely independent judgment, not a fixed number of role messages. Proposed change: each role defines what it protects, the evidence it trusts, the conditions that require it again, and when that evidence expires. A piece records full contributions only for roles actually called; an inactive Business or Experience role gets one factual non-trigger with its evidence, never an invented carrier, conclusion, or assumptions. An active role returns at the earliest run that can really disprove its conclusion, before the associated choice becomes expensive. Until that run exists, the concern remains unresolved.

**Engineering — carrier `/root/redesign_engineering_role` — active.** Engineering read the current runner, broker, host proof, task checker, and raw host records. The root Product context used seven model turns and Business used three because Product polled through model turns and Business had to discover evidence through extra tool reads. Product consumed 193,835 gross / 31,787 fresh tokens; Business consumed 72,649 gross / 24,265 fresh. The host's base envelope creates a non-zero floor, while stable prompt prefixes make cache reads large. Engineering assumes a role can receive direct source bytes and command output in a narrow, hashed packet without substituting Product's summary for primary evidence. Proposed change: the runner prepares exact path-and-hash evidence packets, launches active roles concurrently, allows one structured response per decision stage, and waits outside Product's model context. Product and Engineering remain distinct; the same Engineering carrier gives its pre-code feasibility conclusion, implements, and returns on the real run. The same active carrier returns within a piece; a new piece starts with a focused context unless the miss-escalation rule requires continuity.

### Product synthesis

The four findings converge on changing the rule, not merely the prompts.

1. Product, Business, Experience, and Engineering remain four distinct responsibilities with different primary evidence. Separate context is still mandatory whenever a role is called; four headings from one context still fail.
2. All four contribute at initial Shape, whenever a wrong promise reopens Shape, and at the first Map. The same still-current carriers may resume with only the changed evidence for that Map. A later re-map calls only roles whose evidence, protected concern, or piece ordering changed.
3. Product and Engineering are separate and active on every substantial implementation piece. Product protects the outcome and integrates one decision but never implements it. Engineering gives its feasibility and safety conclusion before code, owns the implementation, and reports what the running system changed.
4. During Shape, Business and Experience write observable call conditions into `product.md`; during Map, they apply those conditions to the pieces and name the earliest run that could settle each active concern. Business or Experience returns before code only when its condition fires, its evidence is stale, the piece changed since Map, or the role says it is materially affected. Uncertainty counts as fired. Product cannot waive any of those facts.
5. An inactive Business or Experience role has no carrier and contributes no departmental prose. The piece records only the exact condition that remained false and the evidence showing that. If scope changes or the factual basis is missing, Product must call the role.
6. An active role returns at the earliest run that can disprove its named conclusion, before the related choice becomes expensive. A technical run commonly settles Engineering; observed use settles Experience; real adoption, value, and operating evidence settle Business. A concern with no informative run remains unresolved rather than being marked handled.
7. Business's evidence-backed `kept / broken / not judged` ruling remains binding for business-changing pieces and every milestone. Contributor exclusion, preserved consequential dissent, specialist exit triggers, the miss-escalation rule, and the small-change exemption all remain.
8. Existing pre-v6 products receive one full separated assessment before their next substantial piece. Historical work is never backfilled. Shape reopens only for a wrong promise; Map reopens when the assessment changes the pieces or their order.

The execution also changes. Active roles receive narrow packets containing the exact primary source material, paths, hashes, and command output they need. Product's interpretation is not a replacement for that evidence. Independent contexts run concurrently where ordering permits; the host waits and measures outside Product's context; no model spends turns polling. Within a piece, one carrier takes a role from its contribution to its informative return. A fresh focused context starts the next piece unless continued involvement was triggered.

The failed 250,000-gross-token run remains failed. The redesign does not relabel cache reads as free or move that old limit. Before another complete run, isolated stages must predict the whole path at no more than 200,000 fresh tokens, leaving twenty percent below a 250,000-fresh hard stop. Host evidence reports gross, cached, and fresh tokens separately; fresh means uncached input plus output. A separate 750,000-gross emergency stop catches runaway history or polling, and the 900-second wall remains. The complete cross-cutting fixture still targets at most 250,000 gross tokens; exceeding that target is a Business cost finding even when the fresh-token stop did not fire. No end-to-end run starts until the role selection controls and isolated stage envelopes pass.

### Preserved dissent and open evidence

- Gross tokens represent context processed and remain relevant to latency and operational drag; fresh tokens better isolate marginal model work when large prompt prefixes are cached. The redesigned proof reports both. The exact long-run commercial weighting is not yet earned, so Business judges the observed combination rather than letting either number alone declare value.
- Reusing a carrier saves reorientation but can accumulate history and bias. The synthesis permits continuity inside one piece and across the first Shape-to-Map delta, where the evidence is explicitly current. It starts fresh on later pieces unless a miss or unresolved concern makes continuity itself valuable.
- Selective staffing can recreate the original missing-lens problem if Product writes or silently interprets the conditions. Business and Experience therefore author their own observable conditions, apply them at Map, and can activate themselves later. A false inactive decision is a planted control and a consequential miss widens the condition.
- Kernel fixtures can prove selection, independence, cost, and changed actions. They cannot prove lasting value in a real product. `capabilities.md` must keep that gap open until the first safe production adoption.

### Proposed replacement promise — not yet ratified

> **Speck builds substantial work with four protected product lenses, and separates each lens when it can change the product. Product, Business, Experience, and Engineering first assess the product from distinct contexts when its promises and initial build order are set. Business and Experience define observable conditions that require their return. Every substantial piece then has separate Product and Engineering carriers; Business and Experience join before code when their condition fires, their evidence is stale, the piece changed, the answer is uncertain, or they declare a material effect. Product cannot waive those facts. An inactive role produces no fake report — the piece cites the condition and evidence that kept it out. Product integrates one decision and never implements; Engineering owns implementation. Each active role returns at the earliest real run that can test its conclusion, while the choice is still cheap, and unresolved evidence stays visible. Business still gives a binding ruling on business-changing work and every milestone. A missed condition broadens itself and forces the role into the next comparable piece; a repeat keeps it involved through the milestone. Role contributors never count as fresh testers or judges, specialists still need one responsibility and an exit trigger, small changes remain exempt, and the owner still receives one product recommendation rather than departmental reports.**

This replaces the universal “all four at every piece and all active roles at the first run” cadence. It does not weaken distinct contexts, role-owned evidence, Product/Engineering separation, Business's gate, reviewer independence, or the response to a miss.
