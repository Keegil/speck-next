# What the fourth campaign taught the kernel

**The campaign:** the pilot's instrument-net piece — the instruments themselves put under a hearing. One meta-board over every check, four honest states, and the question the last three campaigns kept circling: can the things that certify the product be trusted? The arc ran in one day: an adversarial experiencer broke the net twice, a mid-hearing fix batch turned every lived attack into a standing control, a scoped re-walk confirmed the closures and found one residue of its own, two blind judges ruled LAND convergently, and the owner's grade on the landing ask produced learning 4 the hard way. The primary record lives in the product's own private repo; this file carries only what generalizes.

**Outcome sought:** the next campaign's hearings find defects by running, keep the attacks they survive, and never send the owner an ask he has to translate.

**How I'll know it works:** at the next machinery-piece hearing, an experiencer record quotes executed commands and their output; a closed finding's own reproduction stands as its watching control; a re-walk reports its free swing; and no owner ask ships without its one-sentence product reading written first.

---

## What the campaign proved first

The piece loop held at its most self-referential: a hearing about the hearing's own instruments, with the receipt gate, the Built-same-commit rule, blind convergent judges, and operative rulings all executed as written. And the label-matches-status law caught its own author twice in one hearing — the work file's «closed» and the contract's implied trust both overclaimed, and both were caught by parties that did not write them. The law needs no change; it needs exactly the decorrelation it already demands.

## Learning 1 — machinery pieces need an experiencer who attacks by running

The experiencer that broke the net was not a persona walking a screen — it was an adversarial operator who ran the boards and then tried to fool them: it gutted a check to `process.exit(0)` and watched the meta-board call it GREEN, and forged a row into a raw-evidence table and watched the containment hash stay byte-identical. Two real breaches in one hour, on code that four earlier rounds of reading-based review had passed. This is the run-medium axis doing what reading structurally cannot.

The kernel's form, one law in the experience skill: **a piece whose product is machinery (checks, boards, pipelines — anything with no human surface) names at least one experiencer who attacks by running, and its record quotes executed commands and their output — a machinery-experience record with no executed attack is a read, not an experience.**

## Learning 2 — a survived attack is kept, verbatim, as the standing control

The previous campaign established that a repair ships with its instrument. This one sharpened it: the strongest instrument for a lived finding is the finding's own reproduction, preserved. Every attack from the hearing now stands as a planted control the meta-board fires on demand — the exact forged-row probe, the exact silent-exit board — not approximations of them. An approximation of an attack tests the approximation.

The kernel's form: **when a hearing's finding closes, the closure keeps the finding's own reproduction as the watching control wherever the reproduction can be kept; a closure watched only by a new, paraphrased check says so.**

## Learning 3 — the scoped re-walk carries one free swing

The route-back law says re-experience against the exact scenarios the judgment named. This campaign's re-walk did that — and was also licensed to try one variation of its own choosing. The only new finding of the entire back half (a board that prints its exact proof line while doing no work still reads green) came from that one licensed swing, not from the named scenarios. Exact-scenario re-walks confirm; the free swing is what keeps the adversary powered.

The kernel's form: **every scoped re-walk includes one skeptic's pass beyond the named scenarios, chosen by the re-walker, and reports its result either way — a re-walk that only confirms was only asked to confirm.**

## Learning 4 — the owner ask gets a producer, not another rule

The conductor law already says product words, never method words — and the landing ask still went out as «ratify or strike the containment widening», and the owner's grade came back in the plainest possible words: he always needs the actual product implications. The rule detected nothing until he fired; rules detect, producers produce. The whole ask reduced to one sentence that was only written after the grade: «should the checks also guard the raw receipt data, or only the beliefs built on it?»

The kernel's form, in the conductor section: **before any owner ask ships, write the one product sentence it reduces to — what changes for the product's users under each option — and put that sentence first; an ask whose product sentence cannot be written is not ready to send. The options and descriptions of a question tool are part of the ask and carry the same law.**

## Learning 5 — a judge who re-runs outranks a judge who reads

Both judges re-took every checkable claim at their own hand before counting a verdict — reran the boards, the control mode, the census, and wrote their own residue queries rather than trusting either record's read-back. Their four verdicts cite their own runs. The judge skill already lets a judge send an experiencer back for another run; this is the cheaper, stronger form for claims the judge can check directly.

The kernel's form, in the judge skill: **a judge re-takes checkable claims at their own hand where the surface allows it; a verdict standing only on someone else's record of a run says so in the ruling.**

## Candidate for the dev suite, not a law

The meta-board pattern itself generalizes: every check reports one of four states — green · red · blocked (crashed) · did-not-exist-this-run — plus a work signature (a board's own tally line with a floor, so exit-0-did-nothing reads red) and, where checks read a store, a containment gate over a *discovered* population, never a hand-kept list. Any Speck Next repo's proven-means checks can die silently today. This is dev-suite material: an adoptable shape, with its named scope stated honestly (the signature is self-reported; a deliberately forged tally is review-layer territory).
