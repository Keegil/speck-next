The owner requested necessary Speck Next changes after a read-only investigation of recent Odd development. The original goal already required the complete product and visual quality. Compression into the active goal omitted specific experience requirements; reviewers saw UI and rejected increments, but some reading-burden findings were treated as optional or owner taste. ## The change

The existing entrypoint now supports preparing a usable goal from the request and authoritative records, and comparing the registered objective with those sources after shortening or transfer. Specific experience requirements survive alongside scope, authority and budgets. A setup milestone cannot stand in for the product outcome. Existing host continuation is reused; no scheduler or goal service is added.

Resume guidance considers unresolved experience findings and the whole affected job when a repair chain grows. Craft calls for implementing a coherent alternative when local tweaks leave the overall experience weak. Experience chooses representative content states; Judge classifies readability by consequence for the agreed job and limits narrow acceptance to its actual scope. The optional state template preserves rejected and unresolved work.

No new skill, installed file, mandatory record, fixed roster, approval flow or spending authority is introduced. The installed file ceiling remains unchanged.
