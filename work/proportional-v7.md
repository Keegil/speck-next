September 25, 2026. Consumer: builders receiving ordinary user requests after installing or upgrading to v7. Product owner: Kjetil. Product integration: Codex `/root`.

## Owner request

> Bro! I need Speck Next to be a lot more proportional and apply a lot more judgment in terms of which parts of the methodology to apply and how "hard" to apply them based on the intents and tasks it receives from the user. Let's knock out a new major version which remedies this. And let's not spend a fuckton of time and tokens doing it. ## Outcome and handoff

Each request enters at its actual scope. Choose clarification, planning, records, expertise, verification, and review separately by intent, uncertainty, consequences, reversibility, and continuity. The five skills remain available tools; the five phases cease to be mandatory checkpoints. Missing documents do not force a project kickoff. A small new project can reach working output from a short brief. A small risky change receives relevant protections without unrelated ceremony. Scale effort down when evidence settles the concern. User budgets constrain the work; never silently substitute an incomplete result or spend indefinitely.

Keep real use, honest evidence, owner control over consequential choices, independent review for substantive results, complete-job ownership, and repair/re-run of concrete failed behavior. A bounded independent reviewer may exercise the result and judge it in the same fresh context. Add further specialists, independent judges or user perspectives for distinct material uncertainties, rather than a fixed roster. A trivial reversible change may use a focused check without summoning review. Never manufacture independent evidence when another context is unavailable.

One Engineering owner integrates the method and installer. Method implementation is a bounded Cursor Composer task. Product maintains product/map/decision/state/work records; it does not edit the distributed method. Upgrade preserves existing product records and unresolved work byte-for-byte; v7 retires earlier mandatory method ceremonies, including team assessments, without declaring their product findings resolved. Unknown or unsafe filesystem states still refuse safely. Keep the proven transaction machinery.
