# What v3's first full mapping taught the kernel

**The run:** Pilot, 2026-08-19 — shaping exit through map ratification in one day, rounds 12–22 of its `work/mapping.md`, under the v3 dispatcher and the `map-build` skill for the first time. The owner steered eleven distinct beats and closed with *"This is the plan!"* — the same owner whose verdict on the pre-v3 mapping was *"extremely unstructured and haphazard."* Every claim below is scoped to this one campaign, and the conductor across all its rounds was one agent, which is a correlation the kernel should keep in mind reading it.

**What held and should not be touched.** The spine did its job under real traffic: the dispatcher read the artifacts and refused a mapping the owner was ready to start (his "Let's get to mapping!" was enthusiasm, not the missing ratification — the exit conditions caught it, not tact) · receipt-at-dispatch went three for three, and the third receipt's independent re-derivation caught real drift the author's own green test had missed · the completion test failed twice with teeth before it passed, both times on checks the author believed green · rule 8 absorbed an owner re-cut *after* the map review without the record lying anywhere · and round 11's blank-the-cut-keep-the-record, plus the licensed after-the-fact comparison, recovered a whole piece the fresh cut had missed (the learning loop) — the blanking discipline and the comparison license compose, and both earned their place.

## Finding 1 — a receipt that names no owner binds nobody. Bitten once; the fix held three times.

The shaping review's receipt said *"Reviewer: … this session"*. The session that opened it went quiet; a second session read the empty Findings section as "never ran," amended the receipt, and dispatched — while the first session's reviewer was also running. Two reviewers executed one dispatch in parallel, neither knowing of the other. The accident paid this time — the two converged blind on six of ten findings, the strongest decorrelated confirmation in the repo — but the mechanism was luck, and the duplicate cost a full reviewer run. Every receipt after that carried two lines: *owner of this run* (which session, dispatching when) and *what a later session does on finding the Findings empty* (re-dispatch under its own named line, never assume). Three receipts ran under that form without collision. Smallest form: those two lines join the receipt spec in `independent-review`.

## Finding 2 — the ratification ask must be a translation, and the translation is a defense layer, not politeness

The chain, verbatim at both ends. The ratification ask arrived as a paste of `map.md`'s own dialect — slugs, milestone names, supersession talk. The owner: *"You are so fucking convoluted and jargony and you assume so much context that it's infuriating. I can't ratify something you are explaining in a foreign language! Who talks like this??"* The plan was re-rendered in product words (round 20). **In the very next round, reading that rendering, he caught what three decorrelated refuters and the independent map reviewer had all held green** (finding 3 below). The conductor law already says product words, never method words; what this run shows is *why that law reaches the exit conditions*: the owner is the only reviewer who reads for the product's whole properties by taste, and a ratification given on prose he cannot parse is a signature, not a judgment. Worth weighing as kernel text: the exit's "owner ratified in his own words" presumes a rendering he could read — the artifact is linked, never pasted, and the ask is made on the translation.

## Finding 3 — a piece's charter can license violating a standing ruling while every enumerable check passes

The fresh cut created a foundation piece chartered *"the counting floor: no model, no screen"* — and assigned it families, dish-reading and dating, three judgments the owner's recorded ruling explicitly gives to the model (*"Why are you even building these ratios and thresholds and constants? Why aren't you outsourcing the judgment to the MODEL…"*). A no-model piece owning model-judgment work is a license to rebuild exactly the heuristic dead end that ruling was written against. Three refuters attacked the cut from three references: green. The independent map reviewer re-derived the whole completion test: green — it even *moved a property token out of the piece* because "no model runs there," bookkeeping the symptom instead of catching it. The completion test checks token placement, frame partition, proven-means presence; none of its rows can see a charter contradicting a ruling. The owner saw it in one read of the plain rendering: *"It will very quickly turn into everything but AI first."* Failure shape for the kernel to weigh, in its smallest form — options include pieces stating their effect on the whole-product properties the way decision entries already must, or the map review's form gaining one lens that reads each piece's charter against the standing rulings. The evidence is the point; the design is the kernel's.

## Finding 4 — round numbers collide across records, and citations go phantom

Pilot's `decisions.md` carries entries titled "Round 31–35" (2026-08-17, reconstructed); its `work/shaping.md` reached rounds 31–32 on 2026-08-19 with different content, after explicitly ruling that renumbering an append-only record would be a second wrong. Result: the map cited "round 31/33/35" for rulings the shaping record holds unnumbered, and the reviewer found a future agent would land on the wrong round or nothing. Smallest form: a citation names the record and date, never a bare round number — or rounds carry record-scoped ids. One line of convention either way.

## Two small things worth keeping from the reviewer's own report

The map's accounting paragraph drifted from the piece texts it summarized in three places, and in each the piece text was the more careful document. The reviewer's suggestion stands on its own: *treat the accounting as derived output and generate it, rather than hand-maintaining it beside the prose.* And the extended checks this campaign added under review pressure — per-piece text cross-checked against the accounting, the template's risky field actually tested, the artifact census given a stated population instead of a numerator-equals-denominator ratio — all caught real defects and could fold into the kernel's completion test.

## Open

One campaign, one day, one conductor. Finding 1's fix is measured only three receipts deep. Finding 2's causal claim (plain rendering → owner catch) has exactly one instance. Finding 3's design space is deliberately left open — the kernel has been rightly reluctant to grow artifact classes, and a lens on an existing review is cheaper than a new gate. Nothing here is a patch; it is this campaign's evidence, filed for the kernel's own loop.
