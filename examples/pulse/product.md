# Pulse

You notice what your energy is doing — without an app, an account, or any habit heavier than typing one word.

**Who it's for:** one person who suspects their energy has a pattern and wants to see it, not manage it. Today they'd use nothing, a notes app they abandon, or a wellness app that wants their email and their attention.

**Jobs:**
- *log:* "Capture how I'm running today, in under a second, so the record exists without becoming a chore."
- *see:* "Show me my last two weeks at a glance, gaps and all, so I can trust what I'm looking at."
- *notice:* "Tell me something true about my pattern that I hadn't put into words — from my own data, in my own language."

**The honest free comparison:** pasting a journal into any chatbot gets a decent reflection. What that path costs is exactly what Pulse refuses to cost: your health data leaves your machine, and the friction means you'd do it once and never again. Pulse is not a paid product; it's an example. But its wedge is real:

**The wedge:** private by construction and already where your data is. *(claim: local-model — the insight comes from a model running on this machine; the journal never leaves it. Fires on the real shipped path: a canned or templated "insight" would break the product's core promise and is treated as lying.)*

**We are not:** a tracker with goals and streak-shame (that's the app they abandoned) · a chat you have to drive (the model speaks when asked, once, briefly) · a diagnosis machine (it notices patterns; it never names conditions).

**Whole-product properties:** the chart never invents data · a logged day is never lost · the model's words always trace to real logged values · calm — nothing nags, nothing gamifies.

**Magic moment — `moment: the-noticing`.** Surface: `pulse innsikt`. Trigger: the user asks, once, after logging for a while. Beats: a short wait it's honest about → one concrete observation citing their real days and values → one gentle question or suggestion, take it or leave it. It must feel like a sharp friend glancing at your fortnight and saying the true thing — not a horoscope, not a coach. Proving scenario: a journal with a real planted pattern (recurring low Mondays); the observation must name that pattern with the actual days, and a journal with no pattern must NOT produce a claimed pattern.

**Feel:** trygg, rolig, tørrvittig-vennlig. Feels like a wise colleague's aside, not a wellness app's cheer. Restraint rules: no exclamation marks · no emojis · never more than four sentences of insight · waiting is named plainly ("tenker – dette tar rundt ett minutt"), never spun. Cheapeners: streak celebration, motivational quotes, percent scores.

**Banned in front of the user:** "optimalisere" (say what to try instead) · "din reise" (it's a fortnight, not a journey) · "AI-drevet" (the user doesn't care) · any diagnosis word (angst, depresjon, utbrenthet — it notices, it never names conditions).

**The AI surface (`pulse innsikt`):** the machine counts, the model phrases. Pulse computes the weekday pattern deterministically — and a pattern only counts when it beats 200 shuffles of the user's own values, so chance never gets narrated. Only the computed truth goes to the model, whose sole job is warmth: restate the observation (≤ 2 sentences) and ask one gentle question or propose a small experiment (≤ 2 sentences), Norwegian. Every weekday and number in the model's words must exist in the computed fact — nothing invented, enforced mechanically. Good: "Mandagene dine ligger på 2 i snitt, lørdagene på 5. Er det noe søndagene gjør med deg — eller noe mandagene mangler?" Unacceptable and mechanically rejected: any day or number not in the computed fact (fabrication — the worst defect) · banned or diagnosis language · emoji · missing question · echoed instructions. When there is no statistically honest pattern: "Ingen tydelige gjentakende mønstre ennå", instantly, without calling the model. When the model fails or its words break the rules: the user sees the computed truth stated plainly — their real data, no gentle question, never a canned or invented insight.

**Crunch order:** honesty of the chart and journal holds above everything; the insight flexes (it can say it has nothing); polish defers.
