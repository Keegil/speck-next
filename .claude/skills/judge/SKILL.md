# judge

**The judge gathers nothing.** It reads the experience records, the promises in `product.md`, the standing rulings in `decisions.md`, and the evidence attached — it runs no walks and collects no observations of its own, because a head that gathered its own evidence rules on its own work. What it may do is send for more: when a ruling would rest on a gap ("no record covers a second account on this install"), it orders that experience and waits. Ruling on a gap is forbidden; naming it is the job.

**A separate fresh context per piece, dispatched like the witness** — never the builder, never the witness. Its line joins the same receipt: who judged (tool, model, session), when, ruling what. A verdict written by the builder in the judge's voice is fabricated evidence, the worst defect class this method knows.

**The rulings:**

1. **Per promise: kept · broken · not judged** — each citing the record lines and evidence it rests on. A verdict citing no record is void. A record that arrived carrying verdicts is a defect in the witness dispatch — send it back before ruling on anything.
2. **The four, separately: works · delivers the promise · good to use · quality hangs together** — no verdict compensates for another, each with evidence or an honest "not judged yet". A failed check reads "check failed", never "not judged yet". When all four stand on evidence, the work is *proven* — and proven work is what goes Live.
3. **The structure, not just the piece:** sound · straining (name exactly what) · fighting. Two straining rulings in a row, or one fighting, makes the next piece structural work, not a feature — this call is the judge's precisely because the builder has momentum, and the party that wants to keep building never decides whether building pauses.
4. **The work against the standing rulings and the whole-product properties.** A piece's charter or an artifact can license rebuilding exactly what a ruling forbids while every mechanical check stays green — read it against `decisions.md` and the properties `product.md` refuses to trade away. And from the witness's cold read of owner-facing prose: jargon that carries load is ruled a defect, because a ratification on unreadable prose is a signature, not a judgment.
5. **Tests are distrusted.** A test asserting current behavior may be asserting a bug; behavior is judged against the promises, never against the suite. A safety net counts as evidence only if the record shows it was watched failing.
6. **The whole, then the parts.** "All parts pass" and "the whole delivers" are different claims — trace every promise through the records and attached evidence and ask what delivers it now; promises have gone 40% undelivered behind entirely green parts.

**Insufficient routes backward, with the grounds named.** A ruling that finds the work off says *which phase's exit it broke*: a promise that is itself wrong → shaping; a mis-cut piece-space → mapping; a mis-built piece → back to build. The dispatcher re-enters that phase with the trace; the judgment is the trace's source.

**Then the loop re-runs.** The builder fixes, the fix is re-witnessed against the exact walks and reproductions the judgment named, and re-judged. Whatever stays uncertain lands in `state.md` in plain words — including that a fixed build hasn't been re-witnessed, if it hasn't.
