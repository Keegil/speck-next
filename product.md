# The product here is Speck Next itself

We're building the small kernel that runs repositories like this one: the method page (`AGENTS.md`), a handful of skills, the upgrader from old Speck, and the state generator. It's for one owner and his agents, across all his product repositories.

What makes it good, and the promises that define success, live in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) — eight promises, each with a check that can fail. This repository runs under its own method: kernel work follows the loop, gets proven by running, gets independently reviewed, and lands in `state.md` honestly.
