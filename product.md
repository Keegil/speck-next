# The product here is Speck Next itself

We're building the small kernel that runs repositories like this one: the method page (`AGENTS.md`), a handful of skills, the upgrader from old Speck, and the state generator. It's for one owner and his agents, across all his product repositories.

What makes it good, and the promises that define success, live in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) — eight promises, each with a check that can fail. A great running product comes first. Owner attention, elapsed time, and model cost then tell Business whether the method earned its place and which sufficient path to prefer; a token estimate cannot erase valid product evidence. Once a real result exists, only a concrete product finding or a new product claim can authorize another build—method and cost findings stay visible but never create duplicate product work. This repository runs under its own method: kernel work follows the loop, gets proven by running, gets independently reviewed, and lands in `state.md` honestly.

## Product team

- **Product** protects the owner's result-first product-building intent, the eight contract promises, and the order in which the kernel earns them. It reads the owner's words, this file, the map, decisions, running evidence, and the other roles' distinct conclusions. Product keeps the product, map, decisions, and work records true, integrates one recommendation, and never implements the installed method. Product is active on every substantial piece.
- **Business** protects useful adoption, owner attention, operating cost, and durable method value. It trusts observed adoption, measured host usage, elapsed time, owner interruptions, maintenance cost, and running product value—not a claimed business case. It judges cost against the product difference fresh people actually experience; an exceeded estimate is a finding, while exhausted execution authorization stops further spend. Business is called when a piece can change who adopts Speck Next, the adoption path, owner attention, context/token/time cost, ongoing maintenance, or the value that makes the method worth keeping. Its evidence expires when the host, model, runner, method cadence, or affected product path changes enough that the last measured run no longer represents the decision. Business is always active at Shape, the first Map after Shape, business-changing pieces, and milestones.
- **Experience** protects the owner's and builder's ability to understand, steer, and enjoy the method without carrying its internal machinery. It trusts observed owner and builder journeys, the actual installed words and templates, real product use, and the declared feel—not role-authored claims about users. Experience is called when a piece changes anything the owner or builder sees or does: method wording, sequence, handoff, template, role-selection flow, user-facing surface, comprehension, accessibility, trust, or feel. Its evidence expires when one of those surfaces or flows changes. Experience is always active at Shape and the first Map after Shape.
- **Engineering** protects feasibility, safety, reversibility, host behavior, migration, and release integrity. It reads the repository, runtime, dependencies, driver events, fixtures, and real command output. Engineering owns the installed method, templates, installer, migration, development suite, and release implementation. It keeps the product running inside prospectively declared authorization for contexts or model turns, elapsed time, retries, owner interruptions, and pre-run reading. Engineering is active on every substantial piece.

At piece setup and whenever scope or evidence changes, Product rechecks the Business and Experience conditions against direct evidence. A fired condition, missing or expired evidence, a listed material change, or uncertainty calls the role; Product cannot waive it. The role may also declare itself materially affected when present. A false inactive decision stops and repairs the current piece, broadens the condition, and keeps that role active on the next comparable piece.

Every active role names the observation that would change its conclusion and the earliest run that can reveal it, then returns at that run before the piece lands or the choice becomes expensive. Engineering includes its run evidence in the build handoff but cannot stand in for another role's direct evidence. A due Business ruling still blocks business-changing work and every milestone. Product remains the owner's one interface.

There is no standing specialist. A specialist joins only when a product-specific risk gives it one responsibility and an observable exit trigger.
