# The v1 harvest

**Outcome:** the parts of old Speck that made products stand out — product shaping (jobs-to-be-done, first principles, AI-first bar, magic moments), experience and taste judgment, and the remaining scar lessons — live in the successor as a shaping skill and review references, distilled to plain language.

**How I'll know it works:** a real product gets shaped and built end to end with the new skills (including one with a real AI model on the shipped path), and a strict gap-check against old Speck's lesson stores finds nothing valuable left behind.

**Result (2026-08-13):** landed. `shape-product` carries the 18 questions that mattered with each one's scar, plus the eight shaping rules (outcome before features, the 2126 question, differentiators fire for real, magic moments name their proof, honest-before-priced, whole-product properties, short-is-decided, the file passes its own bans). `independent-review` gained the experience reference (the walk, three verdicts, taste-without-tyranny) and five scar additions to the attack playbook (mirror sweep, self-grading check, persistence read-back, tool-blindness to visual fit, behavioral AI testing). The 21-lesson adjudication (carried / should-carry / skip-with-reason) is in the repo history. Proof-in-use: pulse's product.md was reshaped with the skill and the innsikt increment was built and hardened under it.

**Round 3 (2026-08-14, the owner's AI-first refinement):** the first cut of the "2126" doctrine was surface-level — infer-then-confirm on every screen. The owner corrected it to what he actually means: conceptual reconstruction of the category. Most categories are built from fossils — structures that exist only because software couldn't think (pre-programmed workout plans compiling expertise into artifacts; grocery apps inheriting aisles from buildings). The shaping skill now forces the deeper sequence: find the best human who ever did this job and take that relationship as the bar · interrogate every inherited concept for fossilhood · rebuild from the domain's first principles and the user's real mental model · put a living model of the user at the center that infers, asks, discloses assumptions, and takes corrections · and the collapse test: unplug the model, and if the product still works, the AI was decoration. Infer-then-confirm survives as a consequence, not the rule.

**Round 2 (after the v1 audit):** three misses the audit caught, all landed — the external-dependency round-trip rule (the predecessor's fatal lesson) now in `AGENTS.md`'s build step; a build-time craft anchor (`craft` skill, distilled from old visual-quality) between declaring feel and judging it; and a learning-capture line in the leave-state-clear step so a real product's scars route back into its own files.

**Open:** the experience reference hasn't been exercised on a browser-UI product under this kernel yet (pulse is terminal-only) — noted in state.md as deferred past v1's greenfield-reboot target, where the first real UI story will exercise it immediately.
