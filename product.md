# The product here is Speck Next itself

We're building the small kernel that runs repositories like this one: the method page (`AGENTS.md`), a handful of skills, the upgrader from old Speck, and the state generator. It's for one owner and his agents, across all his product repositories.

What makes it good, and the promises that define success, live in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) — eight promises, each with a check that can fail. This repository runs under its own method: kernel work follows the loop, gets proven by running, gets independently reviewed, and lands in `state.md` honestly.

## Product team

- **Product** protects the owner's product-building intent, the eight contract promises, and the order in which the kernel earns them. It reads the owner's words, this file, the map, decisions, running evidence, and the other roles' distinct conclusions. Product keeps the product, map, decisions, and work records true, integrates one recommendation, and never implements the installed method. Product is active on every substantial piece.
- **Business** protects useful adoption, owner attention, operating cost, and durable method value. It trusts observed adoption, measured host usage, elapsed time, owner interruptions, maintenance cost, and running product value—not a claimed business case. Business is called when a piece can change who adopts Speck Next, the adoption path, owner attention, context/token/time cost, ongoing maintenance, or the value that makes the method worth keeping. Its evidence expires when the host, model, runner, method cadence, or affected product path changes enough that the last measured run no longer represents the decision. Business is always active at Shape, the first Map after Shape, business-changing pieces, and milestones.
- **Experience** protects the owner's and builder's ability to understand, steer, and enjoy the method without carrying its internal machinery. It trusts observed owner and builder journeys, the actual installed words and templates, real product use, and the declared feel—not role-authored claims about users. Experience is called when a piece changes anything the owner or builder sees or does: method wording, sequence, handoff, template, role-selection flow, user-facing surface, comprehension, accessibility, trust, or feel. Its evidence expires when one of those surfaces or flows changes. Experience is always active at Shape and the first Map after Shape.
- **Engineering** protects feasibility, safety, reversibility, host behavior, migration, and release integrity. It reads the repository, runtime, dependencies, driver events, fixtures, and real command output. Engineering owns the installed method, templates, installer, migration, development suite, and release implementation. Engineering is active on every substantial piece.

At piece setup and whenever scope or evidence changes, Product rechecks the Business and Experience conditions against direct evidence. A fired condition, missing or expired evidence, a listed material change, or uncertainty calls the role; Product cannot waive it. The role may also declare itself materially affected when present. A false inactive decision stops and repairs the current piece, broadens the condition, and keeps that role active on the next comparable piece.

There is no standing specialist. A specialist joins only when a product-specific risk gives it one responsibility and an observable exit trigger.
